Trooper units have a Courage value on their Unit Card. When a unit has a number of Suppression tokens equal to or greater than its Courage value, it is Suppressed. If a unit has a number of Suppression tokens that is equal to or greater than double its Courage value, it is Panicked.

When a unit checks to see if it is Panicked, if there are 1 or more allied [COMMANDER] units with higher [COURAGE] within [RANGE 3], it uses the highest of those [COURAGE] instead of its own.

Some units have a null [COURAGE], or "-". These units can never gain or be assigned Suppression tokens and cannot be Suppressed or Panicked.
