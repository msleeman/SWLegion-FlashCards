When a unit on notched bases with the Mobile keyword makes a move, it skips step 1 of the notched base movement section, rotating the unit leader's base. A unit with the Mobile keyword must make a free compulsory Move action at the start or end of its Make Actions step. A unit with the Mobile keyword cannot make Reverse Moves. If a unit with the Mobile keyword makes more than 1 non-compulsory Move action during its Activation, it may not claim Asset tokens that Activation. Additionally, a unit with the Mobile keyword that has claimed an Asset token can only make 1 non-compolsory Move action during its Activation.
